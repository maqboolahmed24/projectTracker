The bundled avatar illustrations derive from DiceBear's Critters artwork and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. Preserve the [avatar notice](assets/avatars/NOTICE.md), [licence](assets/avatars/CC0-1.0.txt) and pinned source provenance when redistributing them.

## Fonts and dependencies

Inter is distributed through `@fontsource-variable/inter` under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Preserve the licence included in that package when distributing font files. Runtime and development dependencies are pinned in `package-lock.json`; their individual package licences and notices continue to apply. A source checkout does not grant additional rights to separately installed editors or services.

## Current logo and historical references

The current square line logo in `brand/maqbool-launch/assets/maqbool-symbol.svg` is original artwork created for Maqbool and covered by the project licence. Its source and geometry are recorded in [the artwork source note](brand/maqbool-launch/assets/SOURCE.md).

Earlier commits used an adaptation of UK Data Service artwork. That artwork has been replaced in current product assets. Historical commits, screenshots and archived design references may still show it; the project does not claim authorship of the original artwork or grant a licence to that third-party material. Third-party names and source references are retained where needed for truthful provenance. They do not indicate affiliation or endorsement.

Before distributing additional third-party files, retain their licence and attribution and confirm that their terms permit the intended distribution. Do not label unverified reference material as original Maqbool artwork.
